## Business Questions and KPI Framework

This analysis aims to answer the following operational questions:

1. How has demand changed over time, and what impact does it have on delivery performance?
   
   **Why it matters:**
   1. Helps Operations anticipate workload spikes.
   2. Determines whether delays are driven by capacity constraints.
   
2. Which stages of the order fulfillment process contribute most to delivery delays?

   **Why it matters:**
   1. Directly identifies operational bottlenecks.
   2. Provides actionable insights and enables more efficient allocation of operational resources.

3. How do delivery delays impact customer satisfaction?

   **Why it matters:**
   1. Helps quantify the customer impact of operational inefficiencies.
   2. Justifies investment in process improvements.

These three questions were selected because they examine the end-to-end relationship between demand, operational execution, and customer outcomes. Together, these questions examine the relationship between demand, fulfillment performance, and customer outcomes. They move from identifying operational pressure points, to diagnosing delivery bottlenecks, to quantifying the customer impact of operational performance.

## Project Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

1. Identifies key demand patterns and evaluates their impact on delivery performance.
2. Identifies the stages of the fulfillment process contributing to delivery delays.
3. Quantifies the impact of delivery delays on customer satisfaction.
4. Provides actionable recommendations for improving operational efficiency and customer experience.
